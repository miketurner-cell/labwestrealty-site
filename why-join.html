Not licensed yet?

Five steps from here to your first listing.

  1. Take the pre-licensing course and examApproved courses are listed by the regulator, now under Government Services: gov.nl.ca/gs.
  2. Pick your brokerageYour registration is filed through the brokerage you join. Mike can talk it through with you first.
  3. Register with Government ServicesThe regulator issues your licence (gov.nl.ca/gs).
  4. Join NLAROne-time join costs about $1,918 + HST; dues about $253 a month; orientation within 60 days.
  5. Keep it current12 continuing-education credits every 2-year cycle.
