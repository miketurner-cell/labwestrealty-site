What the Numbers Show
Sales in Labrador West fell from 137 homes in 2021 to 71 in 2023, then climbed back to 82 in 2024 and 96 in 2025. The first weeks of 2026 are ahead of the same weeks of 2025, with 19 homes sold. Labrador West is a small market, so a handful of sales can move a year’s total.

What This Means If You Are Selling

A home sold in Labrador West in 2025 spent 53 days on the market on average. In 2024 it was 45 days, and in 2023 it was 72. Pricing a home to match what buyers are paying now is the biggest factor in how quickly it sells.

What This Means If You Are Buying

With 96 homes sold in 2025 and 19 already by early April 2026, buyers in Labrador City and Wabush have a steady choice of homes through the year, but good ones can go quickly. Having your financing ready before you start looking helps.

Homes Sold by Month

October 2025 was the busiest month, with 18 homes sold. February 2025 was the quietest, with 3. In January to March 2026, 6 homes sold in January, 5 in February and 8 in March.

Average Days on Market by Month

In 2025 the monthly average ranged from 32 days (November) to 183 days (March). From January to March 2026 it was 55 days in January, 41 in February and 66 in March. A month with only a few sales can swing a lot.

Timing a Sale or a Purchase
In 2025 most months saw between 3 and 10 sales, with a busy fall (18 in October). If you are thinking of selling, talk to us early so your home is ready when buyers are active.
