Who We Are

Royal LePage Turner Realty (2014) Inc. (“Turner Realty,” “we,” “our”) is a real estate brokerage in Newfoundland and Labrador, with its head office at 204 Airport Blvd, Gander, NL A1V 1L6. This one policy covers our five websites: realestategander.com, avalonrealestate.ca, goosebayrealestate.ca, labwestrealty.com and royallepageturner.com, and the services on them. It was last updated on October 27, 2026.

Our Privacy Officer

Mike Turner, Broker of Record, is responsible for how we handle personal information. Reach him at miketurner@royallepage.ca with any privacy question, to see or correct your information, to withdraw consent, or to make a complaint.

Information We Collect

Information you give us:

  • Your name, email address and phone number when you send a contact, showing, home-value or sign-up form, or ask us something in the chat
  • Property details you give us in a valuation or listing inquiry, and any message you write
  • If you create an account: your email address, your sign-in details, and your saved searches, favourites and alert settings
  • If you turn on Listing Text Alerts: your mobile number, and a record of when and where you agreed
  • What you say or type in the chat, and the audio you record if you use a voice feature
  • What you say on a call to 709-256-7999, which is answered by an automated receptionist

Information collected automatically: your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, how you arrived, and the choices you make on a page. The tools in the table below collect most of it.

How We Use Your Information

  • To answer your inquiry and give you the real estate service you asked for
  • To book and coordinate showings with the listing agent
  • To send the alerts, market reports and updates you asked for
  • To measure and improve our websites and our advertising
  • To follow the law and the rules of our profession

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Cookies and Your Choices

Our own pages keep a few settings in your browser (for example your sign-in, favourites, filters, and the chat conversation for the tab you have open). Google Analytics, the Meta pixel and the Follow Up Boss tracker set their own cookies or identifiers.

Where our cookie notice is shown, those three tools load only after you choose Accept. If you choose Decline, or do not answer, they are not loaded. Your choice is kept in your browser for 12 months, after which we ask again, and you can change it at any time from the “Cookie settings” link in the page footer. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as Decline. Until the notice is switched on for a site, those three tools load when a page opens; you can still block them with your browser settings or an extension.

Listing Text Alerts and Listing Email Alerts

Listing Email Alerts are emails from Royal LePage Turner Realty, 204 Airport Blvd, Gander NL A1V 1L6, about new listings that match what you asked for. Listing Text Alerts are text messages about the same. We send them only if you agree: you tick the consent box on the sign-up (email) or on the text-alert sign-up (text), and we record the type of consent, the time and the page. Message and data rates may apply to texts.

If you only send us an inquiry and do not tick the optional “Also email me updates” box, we rely on the limited consent an inquiry gives, which under Canada’s anti-spam law lasts six months, to contact you about it.

To stop: reply STOP to any text, or click the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every email. You can also switch alerts off, or unsubscribe from all of them, on your account page, or write to our Privacy Officer.

How to Unsubscribe

You may withdraw consent to receive marketing communications at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in our emails or by contacting us directly. For text messages, reply STOP.

Sharing

We share your information with the companies above so they can do their job for us, with the agent who is handling your request, with the Royal LePage network where you ask us to, and where the law requires it. We do not sell it.

Retention

We keep personal information as long as we need it to serve you and meet our legal obligations. Form submissions are kept in our customer system for the length of our business relationship and any legal retention period. You can ask us to delete your information, subject to those legal requirements.

Your Rights

You may:

  • ask to see the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • withdraw your consent, including to marketing, at any time
  • ask us to delete your information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • complain to our Privacy Officer, or to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at www.priv.gc.ca

Security and Children

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no method of sending or storing information is completely secure. Our websites are not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information.
